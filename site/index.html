04 / METHODOLOGY

Validation & quality gates

Data science controls are as important as the ranker architecture when evidence is scarce and the distribution shifts.

Validation threat register

01
Structure-level leakage

Split by tautomer-canonical InChIKey14, and purge the held-out structure's spectral references from candidate channels.

02
Instrument and library shift

Measure by source, adduct and collision-energy settings; public examples are not representative of hidden novelty classes.

03
Validation / leaderboard discordance

Use matched-molecule paired bootstrap deltas and record losses, not just successful hyperparameter probes.

04
Submission integrity

Check exact molecule coverage, duplicate IDs, nonempty ranked slots, 25-candidate maximum and optional chemistry-level duplicates.
